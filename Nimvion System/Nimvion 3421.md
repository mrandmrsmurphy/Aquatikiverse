**Nimvion 3421** is a late-G to early-K main-sequence star at the center of the [[Nimvion System]]. Its photospheric temperature is about 5400 K and its luminosity about 0.80 L☉. The system contains circumstellar dust structures and planets with distinctive atmospheric fogs and aerosols. Local atmospheres dominate surface light filtering on the terrestrial worlds.

The star has a radius of about 1.02 R☉ and a mass of about 0.93 M☉. These values place it on the G9 to K0 boundary. Its spectral output peaks near 540 nm, while local atmospheric aerosols can preferentially attenuate shorter wavelengths in planetary daylight.

Photometrically, Nimvion 3421 shows no detected variability. Calcium H and K emission is weak, consistent with a mature star of about 5.2 billion years.

## Planetary System

The system includes:

- [[Rheomede]] – An oblate warm-Saturn analogue at 0.68 AU
- [[Francesca]] – A temperate, fog-covered terrestrial world
- [[Stygium]] – A dry, sulfurous planet with an arsenic-based biosphere
- [[Nivalis]] – A cold world near the system’s frost line
- [[Aurion and Argyra]] – Co-orbital giants in a tadpole configuration at 4.1 AU
- [[Cryosthene]] – A distant dwarf planet embedded in the Mirrorband
- Saphon Ring, Silver Fringe, and Mirrorband – Circumstellar haze belts and resonant particulate structures

Together these components produce a system shaped by scattering media, reflective structures, and active photochemistry.
