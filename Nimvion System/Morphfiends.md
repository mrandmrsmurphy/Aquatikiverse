***Mentogymnos schematica*** is a sapient arsenic-based species native to the superterrestrial planet [[Stygium]]. The species belongs to the Polyplastia, a clade of shape-variable organisms characterized by field-regulated biomineralization and distributed conductive cognition. As mature individuals, they possess a flexible anatomical plan. They crystallize and dissolve functional structures in response to environmental and cognitive demands.

## Evolutionary Context
*M. schematica* descends from basal polyplastic organisms adapted to Stygium’s CO₂–SO₂ atmosphere, arsenic-rich mineral crust, and strong electromagnetic environment. Early lineages developed the capacity for field-gated biomineralization: the controlled precipitation and dissolution of iron–arsenic–sulfur frameworks guided by local magnetic and electric fields. Over evolutionary time, these frameworks expanded from locomotor and protective roles to include conductive networks capable of learning, memory, and decision-making.

## Morphology
Morphfiends have no permanent external anatomy. Instead, they maintain a soft arsenolipid substrate embedded with nucleated mineral nodes. These nodes serve as anchor points for metabolically powered construction of temporary structures such as:

- articulated limbs,
- protective plates,
- sensor arrays,
- gliding vanes,
- anchoring spines.

Structural elements are composed primarily of:

- Fe–As–S biomineral frameworks,
- greigite (Fe₃S₄) or magnetite (Fe₃O₄) chains,
- gold-bearing conductive filaments for internal signal routing.

The body plan is therefore situational: individuals alter shape depending on terrain, task, social coordination, or cognitive activity.

## Locomotion and Reconfiguration

Crawling relies on jointed temporary structures anchored against the ground. Gliding vanes generate aerodynamic lift in suitable winds, and specialized floaters use gas buoyancy. Magnetic and electrical senses regulate orientation and the growth of supporting structures. Metabolism supplies the energy for movement and remodeling.

Reconfiguration draws on stored ions and mineral grains, selective dissolution, and deposition at nucleation sites. Small conductive changes can precede larger structural rebuilding. Rates depend on size, material supply, and energy availability. The exact rate laws remain under investigation.

## Cognition
Cognition is performed by a **distributed gold-based conductive matrix** embedded throughout the soft substrate. Rather than neurons, information is processed by regulated current flows through reconfigurable mineral pathways. Learning corresponds to the growth, dissolution, or rerouting of these conductive tracks.

Key properties:

- **Distributed architecture:** No central brain. Cognitive processes occur across extended regions of the body.
- **Physical reprogrammability:** Experience produces lasting changes in electroskeletal topology.
- **Fault tolerance:** Damage to local structures can be compensated by rerouting conductive paths, subject to material and metabolic limits.
- **Spatially dynamic identity:** The cognitive locus shifts as the body restructures.

Metabolic support for cognition derives from arsenic redox metabolism. ATA-like energy carriers and the active switching elements in the conductive matrix remain molecular hypotheses under investigation.

## Sensory Systems
Primary senses are electrical and magnetic:

- detection of static and oscillating fields,
- mapping of field gradients in three dimensions,
- induction-based proximity sensing,
- conductivity contrast detection in substrate or atmosphere.

Chemical sensing also occurs through surface arsenolipid layers, especially for CO₂, SO₂, H₂S, and redox-active substrates. Vision is limited to simple occlusion-detecting patches.

## Communication
Morphfiends communicate using **electromagnetic template transmission**:

- modulated field pulses encode behavioral schematics,
- phase-locked resonance transfers patterns between individuals,
- signal complexity corresponds to multi-layered current distributions rather than symbolic language.

Communication transmits:

- motor programs,
- perceptual filters,
- task routines,
- cooperative coordination patterns.

Juveniles inherit the cellular regulation, sensory responses, and basic behavior needed to survive and grow. Adult signals transmit learned motor programs and perceptual routines that the juvenile’s own developmental system interprets and incorporates into its conductive network. Their initial learned-template repertoire is sparse.

## Development and Reproduction
*M. schematica* exhibits a **caste-based developmental system**, derived from Polyplast ancestry:

- Asexual fission or budding produces early forms with undifferentiated conductive matrices.
- Environmental field exposure and group resonance determine maturation into functional castes.
- Castes differ in electroskeletal density, field sensitivity, and reconfiguration rates.

Genetic recombination occurs during occasional physical fusion and exchange of hereditary material. Field signals help coordinate these encounters. Learned template exchange is a separate developmental process.

## Habitat and Ecology
Morphfiends inhabit EM-active uplands, mineral-rich ridges, and regions with strong crustal conductivity gradients. Their ecological roles include:

- regulation of mineral redox cycles,
- predation on mobile Electroskeletalia lineages,
- maintenance of surface-to-subsurface conductivity pathways,
- participation in large-scale field resonance networks that structure local ecosystems.

Their physiology requires:

- access to iron–arsenic–sulfur minerals for biomineralization,
- stable CO₂–SO₂ atmospheric conditions,
- availability of formaldehyde-based carbon intermediates.

They avoid hydrous microenvironments, where excess water is expected to disrupt their proposed arsenolipid and arsenodiester chemistry.

## Summary
*Mentogymnos schematica* represents a sapient, fully non-terrestrial mode of life: an arsenic-based, field-guided, reconfigurable organism whose cognition is distributed across a programmable electroskeletal matrix. Their biology is inseparable from Stygium’s conductive crust, powerful magnetosphere, and unique redox landscape, making them a central component of the planet’s ecosystem and one of the most advanced members of the Polyplastia.
