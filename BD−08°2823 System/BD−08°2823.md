# BD−08°2823

**BD−08°2823**, also **HIP 49067**, is the real K-type host star of the [[BD−08°2823 System]]. The setting adds fictional worlds and adopts an explicitly modified orbit for [[BD−08°2823 c]]. This page distinguishes catalog reports from the numerical reference used in the reconciled system.

## Activity and discovery context

HARPS spectroscopy found variable chromospheric emission in the Ca II H and K lines and activity indicators with a roughly 26-day modulation. This complicates radial-velocity interpretation: changing stellar line profiles must be distinguished from orbital reflex motion.[^hebrard]

The planet search grew out of follow-up of candidate variability in Hipparcos photometry. HARPS established the planetary signals; the discovery did not establish transits or measure planetary radii. The original stellar analysis used a K3V classification, a mass of 0.74 ±0.07 M☉, and an uncertain age estimate. Its classification is compatible with retaining K3–K4 V as the broad setting description.[^hebrard]

## Reference values used in the reconciled system

The current dynamics use **0.74 M☉**, while irradiation calculations use **0.225 L☉**. The latter is an explicit model reference inherited from reconciliation, approximately 5.3% below the 0.237 L☉ catalog report. It is not the central observational luminosity, and the former 0.20–0.25 L☉ range is a model exploration range rather than a measured error bar.

At fixed orbit, adopting 0.237 L☉ would increase stellar flux by approximately 5.3% and stellar equilibrium temperature by approximately 1.3%, with unchanged albedo and redistribution. Those are derived comparisons. Climate calculations should state which luminosity they use; the previously documented reference fluxes have not been silently recalculated.

Metallicity, bolometric output, and stellar activity do not by themselves establish the planets’ oxidation states, ultraviolet spectra, atmospheric loss, or sustainable peroxide production. Those remain separate physical models.
