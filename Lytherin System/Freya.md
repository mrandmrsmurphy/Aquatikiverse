The four inhabited moons retain their distinct cultures within Lytherin’s manufactured architecture. Stellar names Lytherin A/B refer to the stars; the giant is Freya.

Freya is a massive gas giant in the Lytherin star system, orbiting the circumbinary pair of F-type stars that define the system’s luminous and short-lived main-sequence nature. As one of the two great planetary anchors of the Lytherin Empire, Freya serves as both a physical and cultural fulcrum. With a mass of approximately 300 Earth masses and a radius of 10 Earth radii, Freya supports an intricate system of moons, four of which are habitable and densely colonized. These moons—Freya 1 through Freya 4—form the heart of the Empire’s ceremonial half, supplying half the imperial lineage and sharing rotational custody of the migrating capital.

## Physical Characteristics
Freya is a high-mass gas giant with an equatorial radius of approximately 63,700 kilometers and an estimated mass of 300 Earth masses. Its orbit at 3.015 AU places it well within the circumbinary stable zone of the Lytherin system. Its orbital eccentricity is low (~0.004), and the planet completes one orbit every 1052.6 Earth days. Freya rotates rapidly, completing a full rotation approximately every 10 hours.

## Atmosphere and Meteorology
Freya’s atmosphere is stratified, with relatively transparent upper viewing windows and thin photochemical haze. Deeper ammonium-hydrosulfide and water-cloud layers depend on the atmospheric temperature–pressure profile. Ammonia is present chemically but is not presumed to condense into an upper cloud at every temperature. The planet’s coloration is marked by vivid banding: 14 distinct atmospheric bands of reddish, tan, and bluish hues—caused by complex sulfur and hydrocarbon chemistry.

Meteorological activity is intense and continuous, driven by deep convection. Freya exhibits:

- Nested polygonal polar vortices.
- Towering polar aurora curtains.
- Massive and persistent storm systems, including:
  - Reddish phosphine-ammonium storms.
  - Bluish UV-processed hydrocarbon hazes.
  - Chaotic beige-tan eddies and storm mergers.

Equatorial lightning storms are frequently visible even from orbit, especially on the night side.

## Magnetosphere
Freya’s powerful magnetosphere can interact with its major moons; whether each remains inside the magnetopause depends on field strength and the stellar wind. It deflects some stellar-wind particles but can trap energetic particles in radiation belts. Dense moon atmospheres and engineered protection, rather than the magnetosphere alone, support surface habitability; magnetopause extent varies with stellar wind. The moons are tidally locked to their orbital motion around the giant, and the field shapes the radiation environment; gravity and the selected orbital architecture govern their orbits.

## Moon System
Freya possesses four primary moons, designated Freya 1 through Freya 4. Each is approximately 0.8 Earth radii and supports Earth-like gravity (~1g). All four are inhabited and politically integral to the Lytherin Empire. They rotate synchronously with their orbits around Freya due to tidal locking, and their orbital periods are arranged near successive 5:2 commensurabilities and monitored as an engineered chain.

### Freya 1
Freya 1 is the innermost and most prestigious moon, considered the cultural and ceremonial heart of Freya’s domain. It enjoys abundant water, a stable mild climate, and serves as the origin of many noble houses and religious institutions. Its cities are known for towering arcologies made of reflective TiO₂ composites, used extensively in orbital construction and planetary architecture.

### Freya 2
A mercantile and academic powerhouse, Freya 2 maintains inland seas and equatorial trade cities known for their canals and commercial guilds. It is a common source of Empresses in the dynastic rotation and has a semi-republican internal structure that favors confederated merchant rule.

### Freya 3
Cooler and more scientifically inclined, Freya 3 is the site of the Atmospheric Concord’s founding colleges. The surface is dominated by high-altitude plateaus, inland seas, and cold steppes. Freya 3’s population is known for conservative geoengineering practices and a devotion to long-term environmental stewardship.

### Freya 4
Freya 4 is a colder, rugged frontier world with deep forests and storm-tossed coasts. Its people are traditionally independent and politically decentralized. The moon has produced many influential theologians and dissidents, and its farside cultures maintain a strong presence in the Deep Communion tradition.

## Role in the Lytherin Empire
Freya forms one axis of the Holy Couple’s power base in the Lytherin Empire. One member of the ruling pair must always descend from a Freyan house, and the imperial capital alternates between Freya and Frey each approximately 7.335-year Conjunction. The Freya moons represent the ideals of order, reflection, and ceremonial continuity within the broader imperial cosmology.

Each moon has 0.64 M⊕, 0.8 R⊕ and approximately 1 g, requiring an iron-rich interior (bulk density ~6.89 g/cm³). The quoted axes and periods are initial design elements, not invariant osculating values. Short gravitational screens support retaining all nine worlds but do not establish long-term resonance libration or tidal survival. The system’s manufacture accounts for the extraordinary satellite inventory. Slow orbital control may be needed; no fixed perpetual power-free resonance is assumed.

All moons in a cluster receive nearly the same mean direct stellar flux: 1.210 Earth flux at Freya and 0.622 at Frey. Local climates differ through terrain, clouds, albedo, greenhouse inventories, tidal/geothermal contributions and deliberate management. Neither the outermost moon nor a permanent farside is deprived of stellar daylight solely by its location around the giant. Atmosphere and radiation protection are designed together.

## Physical basis

Freya orbits at 3.015 AU with a period of 2.8819 yr (1052.6 days), a mass of 300 M⊕ and a radius of 10 R⊕, and receives 1.210 Earth flux; e = 0.004 gives apsides of 3.00294 and 3.02706 AU. Each adjacent pair of its four moons targets a 5:2 period ratio, and the outermost moon at 5,000,000 km lies at about 0.246 of the cluster's roughly 20.35-million-km Hill radius, a less crowded layout that keeps all four inhabited worlds. Nominal initial eccentricities in the screens are 0.003 and are not claimed to stay constant. With k₂/Q = 0.003 and e = 0.02 the inner moon receives about 1.66 W/m² of tidal heat, which scales as e². The system-wide screens, resonance limits and tidal sensitivity are under Physical basis on [[Lytherin System]].
