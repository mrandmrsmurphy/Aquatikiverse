The inhabited moons form part of Lytherin’s manufactured architecture. The stellar components are Lytherin A and B; the giants are Freya and Frey.

Frey is the outer of the two dominant gas giants in the Lytherin System, orbiting the twin F-type stars that define the system’s high-luminosity but short-lived character. Slightly more massive than its inner counterpart Freya, Frey occupies a colder, more subdued position within the circumbinary architecture. With a mass of approximately 320 Earth masses, it hosts five habitable moons (Frey 1 through Frey 5), each of which contributes to the cultural, religious, and political structure of the Lytherin Empire. These moons form the outer half of the imperial duality, producing half of the royal lineage and serving as the counterpart in the system's rotating capital cycle.

## Physical Characteristics
Frey has an equatorial radius of approximately 63,700 km (10 R⊕) and a mass of 320 M⊕. Its nearly circular orbit at 4.205 AU places it beyond Freya but within a dynamically stable circumbinary zone. Its orbital eccentricity is minimal (0.005), and it completes one revolution approximately every 4.7467 Earth-years. Like Freya, Frey rotates rapidly—approximately every 10 hours.

## Atmosphere and Meteorology
Frey’s visual and meteorological features are more muted than those of Freya. Its atmosphere contains:

- Deep and thick cloud decks of H₂O, NH₄SH, and H₂S.
- Upper atmospheric hazes dominated by hydrocarbons (methane, ethane, acetylene), producing soft photochemical tints.
- Broad but diffuse auroral curtains, less filamentary than Freya’s.
- Large, slow cyclonic systems that are more stable and longer-lived than the chaotic mergers observed on Freya.

Overall, Frey presents a "painterly" aesthetic, with soft banding and a subdued appearance, enhanced by Rayleigh scattering and photochemical hazes.

## Magnetosphere
Frey’s magnetosphere, while slightly weaker than Freya’s, can interact with its moons’ upper atmospheres and support auroral activity. Its extent depends on the stellar wind and field strength; radiation belts can also increase exposure. The moons are tidally locked and orbit in monitored, manufactured configurations; magnetic containment is not their gravitational binding mechanism.

## Moon System
Frey hosts five primary moons—Frey 1 through Frey 5—all of which are habitable and play critical roles in the Lytherin Empire. The moons share an adopted radius of 0.8 R⊕ and mass of 0.64 M⊕ with those of Freya. Adjacent periods target a 5:2 commensurability; this replaces the overcompact 3:2 layout. Resonant phase confinement is an engineering objective, not a result established by period ratios alone. All possess Earth-like gravity (~1g) and varied environmental and cultural profiles.

### Frey 1
Frey 1 is the innermost moon, benefiting from a relatively mild managed climate and a large apparent view of Frey. It is known for aristocratic estates, bioengineered farms, and an ancient nobility that often favors tradition and formal hierarchy.

### Frey 2
The industrial and trade nucleus of the outer system, Frey 2 features dome-covered cities and vast maglev transit networks. It plays a dominant role in the Trade Conclave and frequently produces imperial economic ministers and Chancellors.

### Frey 3
A cold, austere world, Frey 3 is rich in hydrocarbons and minerals. Its stark beauty and introspective culture have made it the spiritual stronghold of the Deep Communion, and its monastic orders wield significant influence across the Empire.

### Frey 4
Frey 4 is characterized by glacial plains and storm-lashed coasts. It retains a feudal socio-political landscape, with a strong emphasis on local autonomy, defensive alliances, and cultural preservation.

### Frey 5
The most distant of the Nine Moons, Frey 5 has haze-darkened landscapes and deep-shadow settlement regions. Life is clustered around geothermal vents and heated arcologies. Its culture emphasizes endurance, mysticism, and resistance to centralization, and it was the site of the Shadow Revolts in C.Y. 214.

## Role in the Lytherin Empire
Frey and its moons constitute one half of the dual sovereignty of the Lytherin Empire. One consort must descend from Freya’s houses and one from Frey’s, with the supplying houses and consort roles rotating under the imperial compact, and imperial authority migrates to a moon of Freya or a moon of Frey each approximately 7.335-year Ascension. The Frey cluster is associated with contemplation, endurance, and resistance to decadence. It embodies the “Outer Sphere” in ceremonial cosmology and holds sacred the themes of shadow, renewal, and stewardship of scarcity.

Each moon has 0.64 M⊕, 0.8 R⊕ and approximately 1 g, requiring an iron-rich interior (bulk density ~6.89 g/cm³). The quoted axes and periods are initial design elements, not invariant osculating values. Short gravitational screens support retaining all nine worlds but do not establish long-term resonance libration or tidal survival. The system’s manufacture accounts for the extraordinary satellite inventory. Slow orbital control may be needed; no fixed perpetual power-free resonance is assumed.

All moons in a cluster receive nearly the same mean direct stellar flux: 1.210 Earth flux at Freya and 0.622 at Frey. Local climates differ through terrain, clouds, albedo, greenhouse inventories, tidal/geothermal contributions and deliberate management. Neither the outermost moon nor a permanent farside is deprived of stellar daylight solely by its location around the giant. Atmosphere and radiation protection are designed together.

## Physical basis

Frey orbits at 4.205 AU with a period of 4.7467 yr (1733.7 days), a mass of 320 M⊕ and a radius of 10 R⊕, and receives 0.622 Earth flux; e = 0.005 gives apsides of 4.183975 and 4.226025 AU. Each adjacent pair of its five moons targets a 5:2 period ratio, and the outermost moon at 8,058,819 km lies at about 0.278 of the cluster's roughly 29.01-million-km Hill radius, a less crowded layout that keeps all five inhabited worlds. Nominal initial eccentricities in the screens are 0.003 and are not claimed to stay constant. With k₂/Q = 0.003 and e = 0.02 the inner moon receives about 5.30 W/m² of tidal heat, which scales as e²; the wider 700,000-km inner moon eases this compared with a closer candidate. The system-wide screens, resonance limits and tidal sensitivity are under Physical basis on [[Lytherin System]].
