# Neptune

Neptune is the outermost planet, a deep-blue ice giant with the fastest winds in the Solar System. It was found by calculation before it was seen.

## Physics and chemistry

**Bulk properties.** Neptune has 17.1 Earth masses and a mean radius of 24,622 km (3.87 Earth radii), a density of 1.64 g/cm³ (the highest of the giants), a 1-bar gravity of about 11.2 m/s² and an escape velocity of 23.6 km/s. It orbits at 30.07 AU in a nearly circular orbit and receives 0.0011 times Earth's sunlight, with a 164.9-year period.

**Atmosphere.** Hydrogen, helium and a few percent methane. Winds reach about 2,000 km/h, even though the planet absorbs so little sunlight; the energy comes from Neptune's own interior, which radiates well over twice as much heat as the planet absorbs. Dark storm spots appeared and vanished between observations, and bright methane-ice clouds ride high above them.

**Moons and discovery.** Triton, Neptune's largest moon, orbits backward, which indicates that it was captured from the Kuiper belt, and it has nitrogen geysers on a surface near 38 K. Irregularities in Uranus's orbit led astronomers to predict Neptune's position in 1846.
