# Earth

Earth is the third planet and the only known world with life. Its liquid-water oceans, nitrogen–oxygen atmosphere, large moon and active interior make it the reference against which every other planet in the vault is measured.

## Physics and chemistry

**Orbit and seasons.** Earth orbits at 1 AU in a nearly circular orbit (e = 0.017), defining the flux unit F⊕ = 1 (about 1,361 W/m² above the atmosphere). Its axis is tilted 23.4° to its orbit, which creates the seasons, and the Moon (about 1/81 of Earth's mass) stabilises that tilt against planetary perturbations. The sidereal rotation period is 23.93 hours.

**Bulk properties.** With 5.972×10²⁴ kg, a mean radius of 6,371 km and a bulk density of 5.51 g/cm³ (the highest of the eight planets), Earth has a surface gravity of 9.8 m/s² and an escape velocity of 11.19 km/s. Its metallic core drives a magnetic field that shields the atmosphere from the solar wind.

**Atmosphere and oceans.** About 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen, with argon and a trace of carbon dioxide (about 0.04% today). Oceans cover about 71% of the surface. The oxygen is produced by photosynthesis; without life, oxygen would disappear from the air within a few million years. Plate tectonics recycles carbon through the carbonate–silicate cycle, a thermostat that has kept the surface liquid for billions of years as the Sun brightened.

## In the Aquatikiverse

[[Aialon]] and [[Valen]] are terraformed Earth-like worlds, built to approximate this baseline (20% oxygen at Aialon, 15% at Valen). Middle Path in the [[Ɬiʔa System]] is the other Earth-like world in the vault. Earth's numbers anchor the flux, gravity and atmosphere comparisons for all of them.
