# Makemake

Makemake is a bright, reddish dwarf planet in the Kuiper belt, covered in methane ice, with one small moon.

## Physics and chemistry

**Bulk properties.** Makemake has about 3.1×10²¹ kg (0.00052 Earth masses) and a mean radius of about 714 km, giving a density of about 2.0 g/cm³ (JPL prints 2.1) and a surface gravity of about 0.41 m/s². Its orbit has a semimajor axis of about 45.6 AU, e = 0.159 and an inclination of 29°, with a period of about 308 years. It rotates every 22.5 hours (0.937 days). The mass comes from the orbit of its moon and has a large uncertainty, so the density is not tightly known.

**Surface.** Methane, ethane and nitrogen ices make the surface bright, and the reddish color comes from processed hydrocarbons. Occultations show that it has no global atmosphere comparable to Pluto's, though a thin local one has not been ruled out.
