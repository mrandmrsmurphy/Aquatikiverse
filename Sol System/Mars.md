# Mars

Mars is a small, cold, dry desert world with a thin carbon-dioxide atmosphere, polar ice caps and the largest volcano and canyon in the Solar System. It once had liquid water on its surface.

## Physics and chemistry

**Bulk properties.** Mars has 0.107 Earth masses and 0.532 Earth radii (3,389.5 km), a density of 3.93 g/cm³ and a surface gravity of 3.73 m/s², with an escape velocity of 5.03 km/s. It orbits at 1.524 AU (e = 0.093) and receives 0.43 times Earth's sunlight, varying between about 0.36 and 0.52 over its eccentric orbit.

**Rotation and seasons.** A sidereal day lasts 24.62 hours, close to Earth's, and the axis is tilted 25.2°, so Mars has Earth-like seasons, twice as long and made more extreme by the orbital eccentricity. Unlike Earth, Mars has no large moon to steady its tilt (the two small moons Phobos and Deimos are too light), so its obliquity wanders chaotically over millions of years and drives the movement of polar ice.

**Atmosphere.** The air is about 95% carbon dioxide at a surface pressure of only about 0.6% of Earth's, too thin to hold liquid water at the surface for long. Without a global magnetic field today, the solar wind has stripped much of the early atmosphere. The planet's red color comes from iron oxides in its dust. Features such as dry river valleys and minerals formed in water show that the early climate was wetter and warmer.

## In the Aquatikiverse

No fictional world is a close Mars analog yet. Mars is the vault's reference for a small, cold world with a thin atmosphere and a wandering axis, and for what terraforming would have to overcome.
