# Eris

Eris is the most massive known dwarf planet. Its discovery in 2005 showed that Pluto was not unique, and led the IAU in 2006 to define "planet" and "dwarf planet".

## Physics and chemistry

**Bulk properties.** Eris has 1.66×10²² kg (0.00278 Earth masses, about 27% more than Pluto) and a mean radius of about 1,200 km, giving a density of about 2.3 g/cm³, a surface gravity of 0.77 m/s² and an escape velocity of 1.36 km/s. Its radius is similar to Pluto's within the uncertainty (JPL gives 1,200 ± 50 km), so its higher density means a larger rock fraction.

**Orbit.** The orbit is eccentric (e = 0.438) and steeply inclined (43.9°), with a semimajor axis of about 67.9 AU and a period of about 560 years. It ranges between roughly 38 AU and 98 AU from the Sun, so its sunlight changes by a factor of about 6.6 over an orbit. At present it is near aphelion. Its moon, Dysnomia, orbits it in about 16 days.

**Surface.** The surface is very bright and dominated by methane ice.

## In the Aquatikiverse

No fictional world is an Eris analog yet; it is a reference for a highly eccentric, inclined scattered-disk orbit.
