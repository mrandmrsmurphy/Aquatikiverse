# Haumea

Haumea is a fast-spinning, egg-shaped dwarf planet in the Kuiper belt with a ring and two moons. It rotates in less than four hours, faster than any other known large body.

## Physics and chemistry

**Bulk properties.** Haumea has 4.0×10²¹ kg (0.00067 Earth masses). Its mean (volume-equivalent) radius is about 715 km, but the shape is a stretched ellipsoid, and the equatorial radius of about 870 km is more than a third larger than the polar radius. The mean density is about 2.6 g/cm³. It orbits at about 43 AU (e = 0.194, inclination 28.2°) with a period of about 283 years.

**Rotation and shape.** A 3.9-hour rotation (0.1631 days) stretches the body into an elongated triaxial ellipsoid. The fast spin and the family of icy fragments that share its orbit suggest a giant collision early in the Solar System. It has two moons (Hiʻiaka and Namaka) and a narrow ring, found when it passed in front of a star in 2017.

**Surface.** The surface is bright crystalline water ice over an interior that is mostly rock (density 2.6 g/cm³).
