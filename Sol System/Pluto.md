# Pluto

Pluto is the best-known dwarf planet, a world of nitrogen, methane and water ices in the Kuiper belt, with a thin atmosphere, a large moon (Charon) and active geology revealed by New Horizons in 2015.

## Physics and chemistry

**Bulk properties.** Pluto has 1.30×10²² kg (0.00218 Earth masses) and a mean radius of 1,188.3 km, giving a density of 1.85 g/cm³, a surface gravity of 0.62 m/s² and an escape velocity of 1.21 km/s. Its orbit has a semimajor axis of 39.5 AU, e = 0.244 and an inclination of 17.2°; it comes as close as 29.8 AU, inside Neptune's orbit, but is protected from collisions by a 3:2 resonance with Neptune.

**Rotation and moons.** Pluto rotates retrograde every 6.39 days (obliquity 119.5°). Charon, with about half Pluto's diameter, is large enough that the barycenter of the pair lies outside Pluto; both are tidally locked, always showing the same faces to each other. Pluto has five known moons.

**Surface and atmosphere.** The surface is mostly nitrogen, methane and carbon monoxide ice over a water-ice crust. A huge nitrogen-ice plain, Sputnik Planitia, convects slowly. The atmosphere is mainly nitrogen with methane, at a pressure about 100,000 times lower than Earth's, and it collapses and refreezes as Pluto moves away from the Sun.
