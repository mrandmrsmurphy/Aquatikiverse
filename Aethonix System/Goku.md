**Goku** is the third planet in the [[Aethonix System]], located at 1.345 AU from its central binary stars. With surface conditions remarkably close to those of Earth, Goku is widely considered one of the most naturally habitable exoplanets yet discovered. It features a dense, oxygen-rich atmosphere, shallow sunlit seas, and a stable global climate — all of which contribute to its extraordinary biological productivity. The planet’s surface is ~84% ocean, dominated by estuarine shallows, reef islands, and archipelagos that support dense, trophically layered ecosystems. Total insolation is slightly below Earth’s (≈0.94 S⊕), but high ocean coverage and shallow seafloors more than compensate in biological output.

Goku’s atmosphere consists of ~74.9% nitrogen, 24% oxygen, 700 ppm carbon dioxide, about 1.029% argon and 0.001% krypton/other trace gases in the dry reference. Surface pressure is ~1.3 atm, enabling efficient oxygen diffusion and supporting highly aerobic biosystems. Methane is below the reference surveys’ reporting threshold in the sampled atmosphere; that is not proof of zero methane in all sediments or microsites. The bulk atmosphere is oxidizing. Goku’s twin moons — informally named Gohan and Goten — occupy nominal 180,000- and ~374,415-km orbits with a period ratio near 3:1, generating complex overlapping tidal forces that structure its coastline ecology and create a rhythmic, planetary-scale “pulse” in water levels and salinity gradients.

Despite its oceanic dominance, land does exist — roughly 15–16% of the surface — in the form of isolated islands, reef-laced island mesas and the single continent-scale Aoshima highland, and biologically rich coastlines. Goku’s axial tilt (~11°) gives rise to mild but regular seasonal variation. Its day length (~24 hours) and total insolation are near-terrestrial, but its unusually shallow seafloors (with 60% of the full surface in open ocean and another 24% in photic-zone shallows) allow solar energy to saturate aquatic ecosystems far more effectively than on Earth. As a result, biological productivity per square kilometer is exceptionally high.

## Biology and Biosphere
Main article: [[Life on Goku]].

Goku's biosphere is explosively diverse, especially in its coastal shallows and tide-driven estuarine flats. Coral-analog forests, photosynthetic sponge-mats, and mobile plant-animals dominate primary production. The planet is home to an array of unusual lifeforms including gliding amphibians, semi-autonomous root colonies, and warm-blooded, hexapodal surface grazers that drift with buoyancy pulses across mirror-bright lagoons. Elevated oxygen and overlapping lunar tides shape metabolism, fire regimes, mixing and ecological opportunity; faster evolution is not inferred automatically from either.

Among its most iconic species are the *silting runners* — gliding amphibians that leap from mangrove-like vegetation and communicate via throat-resonant sonic pulses. Bioluminescence, synchronized reproductive cycles, and limb differentiation are common features across phyla. Floating root-rafts, reef jungles, and photoplankton bloom zones form mobile habitats that reorganize on seasonal timescales, creating a biosphere in constant flux.

## Habitability and Exploration

Early expeditions catalogued bioluminescent signal chains, biosphere diversity and the layered tides. This historical candidate/exploration phase preceded permanent settlement; the world is inhabited at the adopted modern reference epoch, year 3000.

Goku’s gravity, 293-K climate reference, oceanic resources and managed environmental conditions support scientific stations, floating cities, reef-tethered platforms and semi-aquatic agriculture. Its high oxygen partial pressure (~0.312 atm) makes fire management and physiological adaptation meaningful design concerns. “Naturally habitable” does not mean a guarantee of unconditional Earthlike exposure safety or native-food compatibility.

By the 25th century, Goku already hosted over one billion humans. Aoshima, its only continent-scale highland mesa, contains the capital of the same name. A distributed democratic government and the Goku Planetary Council maintain ecological zoning, reef sanctuaries and migration corridors. The Japanese–Hawaiian cultural aesthetic, drifting ocean-current cities, tide-based temporal traditions and Festival of the Conjoined Tides remain prominent. No exact updated population for year 3000 is invented.

The economy includes ecotourism, biotechnology, oceanic protein cultivation, biofluorescent dyes and algal textiles. Semi-planetary fire brigades manage lightning-induced island fires. Low land area makes stewardship, floating arcologies and reef-buffered infrastructure central to settlement rather than signs that it remains uncolonized.

## Physical basis

Goku's two moons, Gohan (0.7 lunar masses, 180,000 km) and Goten (0.4 lunar masses, about 374,415 km), have a period ratio near 3:1 with no libration claim, and a compact source-sized 3:2 hypothesis was unstable in the screens. The 1.3-atm reference has a pO₂ of about 0.312 atm. The atmosphere table and screens are on [[Aethonix System]] under Physical basis.
