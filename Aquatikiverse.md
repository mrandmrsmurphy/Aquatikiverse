# Aquatiki-verse

The **Aquatiki-verse** is a version of our universe but with documented inhabited and colonized planets.  There are several alien species, many of them sentient.

This page is the atlas of every system. New visitors should start at [[Home]].

### Gammal

Gammal lies about 3000 pc from Earth toward J2000 17h46m00s, −29°05′00″, corresponding to roughly 5.3 kpc Galactocentric radius. The earlier Earth-frame tuple (3000, 260, 0) remains an archival map annotation with undefined axes/units; it is not used as an independently reconciled position.
