Preserve the user's important fictional identities, life concepts and intended experiences where possible. Explain consistency difficulties and offer defensible repairs. Distinguish author-selected speculative premises, illustrative assumptions, arithmetic checks, numerical screens and established physical results; do not silently promote one into another.

The user confirmed this workflow during AIOS setup. Their priorities determine which characteristics to preserve; do not infer that scientific reconciliation authorizes replacing a precious concept without discussion. When the user requests analysis without changes, keep it read-only until changes are authorized.

**Why:** The vault is a recreational exercise in integrating and learning sciences. Tradeoffs are part of the learning, and apparent rigor must not disguise unsupported claims or erase the intended world.

**How to apply:** State the issue, what a repair preserves, what it changes and what remains unmodeled. Ask about choices that belong to the user; proceed with approved repairs. Put resulting current facts on the article and the decision/change account in the log. Related: [[project_what_this_vault_is]], [[project_article_and_history_separation]].
