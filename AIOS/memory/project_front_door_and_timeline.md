Home.md sits over four legitimate entry points: physics, chemistry, speculative biology, worlds and stories. Reading paths, Biospheres, Glossary and the Aquatikiverse atlas hang off it. The shared universe timeline and the connections between systems have not been built; the user plans to build them. Until they exist no content page mentions them.

**Why:** visitors arrive wanting one of the four subjects, and the user wants each to be a valid beginning.

**How to apply:** when adding a page, link it into the relevant Reading path; when the timeline starts, add a fifth entry to Home and consider timeline frontmatter (in-universe year numbers are allowed).
