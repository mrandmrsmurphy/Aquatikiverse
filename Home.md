# Home

The **Aquatikiverse** is a set of fictional stars, worlds, biospheres and civilizations built to be scientifically workable: each world's mass, orbit, light, climate and chemistry are calculated, and the speculative parts are marked as speculation. The same pages serve four kinds of visitor. Pick the door that suits you; each leads into the same universe.

## Four doors

### Physics
Stars, orbits, tides, stability and climate. Start with the reference articles [[Stars]] and [[Planets and orbits]], with the real [[Sol System]] as the worked comparison. Every world page has a **Physical basis** section showing the calculation behind its numbers. Extreme cases: the six-star [[Trigeminal System]], the tidally heated worlds of the [[Stellula System]] and [[Methuselan System]], the rogue world [[Skemer]], and the black hole [[Tom (black hole)|Tom]].

### Chemistry
Solvents, redox couples and energy budgets. Start with [[Redox Chemistry]], which gives the method for building a biosphere's reaction set, then see it applied: ammonia–water on [[Azotia]] and [[Aphrod]], liquid sulfur dioxide on [[Thionis]] and [[Stygium]], halides on [[Dysmorph]], and the mineral [[Glaciumite]].

### Speculative biology
Life that runs on something other than Earth's chemistry. The index is [[Biospheres]], which tabulates every biosphere by solvent and metabolism. Good first reads: [[Life on Middle Path]] (a silicon and a carbon biosphere sharing one planet), [[Life on Crying Eye]] (fission-powered Corions), [[Life on Alchemis]] (no bulk liquid) and [[Pumper Beetles]].

### Worlds and stories
Places, peoples and settings. The most developed world is [[Middle Path]], a tidally locked planet shaded by a tethered moon in the [[Ɬiʔa System]]. For peoples and states see the [[Lytherin Empire]], [[Skemer-mense]], [[Dichlorans]], [[Morphfiends]], [[Formoquens]] and [[Pedonpods]], and for the whole map of systems see [[Aquatikiverse]].

## How a world page is laid out

A world page opens with a short account of the place, then a property table (mass, radius, orbit, climate), then sections on atmosphere, surface, life and people where they apply. The properties at the top of each page (shown in Obsidian's Properties panel) hold the same numbers in common units, so worlds can be compared directly. The **Physical basis** or **Model status** section at the end states which values are calculated and which are chosen speculation.
