# Orpheus and Eurydice System

*Post-supernova K0V + neutron star system with three exotic planets*

## System properties

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Age | Old; several Gyr (proposed post-supernova fallback system) |

This system comprises a chemically enriched planetary group orbiting a K0V-type star formed from post-supernova fallback material. A neutron star orbits at a wide distance (>15 AU) as the relic of the progenitor star. Three planets orbit the K-type star, each composed of supernova-enriched material with radically non-terrestrial chemistry, physics, and internal structure.

## Stellar Components

### Orpheus

Main article: [[Orpheus and Eurydice]]

- **Type**: K0V (main sequence)
- **Mass**: 0.79 M☉
- **Luminosity**: 0.40 L☉
- **Effective Temperature**: 5250 K
- **Lifespan**: ~25 Gyr
- **Habitable Zone**: 0.62 – 1.07 AU
- **Notes**: Proposed to have formed from enriched fallback and disrupted-companion material; the detailed history is uncertain.

**Eurydice**

- **Type**: Neutron star with no detected coherent pulsations; former recycling is hypothesized
- **Mass**: 2.0 M☉
- **Radius**: 11 km
- **Magnetic Field**: ~10⁸ G (residual)
- **Spin Period**: Estimated >20 ms; spin history uncertain
- **Estimated separation from Orpheus**: ~20–40 AU
- **Notes**: Original remnant of the supernova; now gravitationally bound as a wide binary. Emits negligible radiation at planetary distances. May still be detectable via residual gamma emission or gravitational lensing.

## Planetary System

### Alchemis

Main article: [[Alchemis]]

- **Orbital Distance**: ~0.4 AU
- **Insolation**: ~2.5× Earth
- **Composition**:
  - Surface rich in lanthanides (Eu, Tb) and platinum-group metals (Pt, Rh, Ru)
  - No surface water; dry catalytic substrates dominate
  - Phosphorescent terrain under UV irradiation
- **Key Features**:
  - Glowing rock fields and high-altitude phosphor clouds
  - Native inorganic catalysis and metabolism using CO, NH₃, and other redox-active gases
  - Self-organizing chemical domains supporting crystalline automata and intelligent Formoquens
- **Biochemistry**: Established native photonic-redox biosphere; intelligent [[Formoquens]]
- **Atmosphere**: Dense, dry, oxidant-rich; volatile-deficient

### Laputa — Magnetic + Crystal World (Colonized)

Main article: [[Laputa]]

- **Orbital Distance**: ~0.63 AU
- **Insolation**: ~1× Earth
- **Composition**:
  - Quasicrystalline crust with aperiodic topologies
  - Convective metallic core associated with >1 Tesla surface magnetic field
  - Floating crystalline mountain ranges suspended magnetically (accepted speculative physics)
- **Magnetosphere**: Strongly influences charged-particle exposure; does not provide complete radiation shielding
- **Civilization**:
  - Human colonists inhabit magnetically stabilized zones
  - Architecture incorporates aperiodic crystal forms and suspended infrastructure
  - Advanced materials science based on ambient topological effects
- **Atmosphere**: Neon-rich, with 21% O₂ at 0.90 atm; clean air is breathable, while dusty regions require filtration

### Dysmorph — Radioactive + Density World

Main article: [[Dysmorph]]

- **Orbital Distance**: ~1.6 AU
- **Insolation**: ~0.15× Earth
- **Internal Heat Source**: Radiogenic; uranium/thorium-rich crust and local fission zones
- **Shape**: Nearly spherical in bulk; distorted geoid and pronounced regional gravitational anomalies
- **Key Features**:
  - Local gravity from ~0.7g to >3g due to Os/Ir compositional variation
  - Natural criticality zones with fission-burst events
  - Tectonically unstable; crustal disruptions and gravity traps
- **Atmosphere**: Dense; contains Xe-135, Kr-85, and other fission byproducts
- **Biosphere**: Established ~300 Myr inorganic halide biosphere, including intelligent Teuthis radiomancia (Squiddies)

## Evolutionary History

The present system is a binary: Orpheus and Eurydice. Its heavy-element abundances and neutron-star companion motivate a proposed history involving supernova ejecta, stellar interaction, and an enriched planet-forming disc. Orpheus’s formation from fallback and disrupted-companion material is an uncertain reconstruction, not a directly observed sequence. The original number and fates of progenitors, mass-transfer episodes, and neutron-star kick are not established.

The three planets subsequently developed distinct environments: photonic-redox life on Alchemis, a microbial water-based biosphere and later human colony on Laputa, and an inorganic halide biosphere on Dysmorph. Generation labels are avoided because the preceding stellar and planetary generations have not been established.
