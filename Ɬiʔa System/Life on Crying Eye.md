# Life on Crying Eye

**Life on Crying Eye** is a native, independently evolved biosphere organized around high-pressure brine/mineral interfaces, natural fission provinces and biological fission systems called **Corions**. It inhabits deep reservoirs and phase boundaries beneath [[Crying Eye]]'s mostly frozen surface, with later descendants extending into shallow ice veins, vent systems and the Cryoplate Wastes.

**Corions are literal.** They generate energy through nuclear fission integrated into living systems; they are not renamed ordinary mitochondria, passive radioactive grains or radiolysis alone. Their evolution and portability are a major speculative biological invention of the setting. Natural geological fission provides the inspiration, while the assumed abundance of present-day provinces and biological incorporation go substantially beyond evidence from Earth.

The organisms remain carbon-bearing but use pressure-dependent organic–mineral, ionic and polymeric chemistry. Their cell analogues are not assumed to be Earth cells with modified proteins. Main deep ecological provinces operate at pressures of order **1–3 GPa**, with other lineages specialized to shallower reservoirs or surface conditions. Pressure shapes phase boundaries, solvent behavior and structural chemistry; it does not by itself make every fluid supercritical or make nuclear reactions biologically manageable.

## Natural fission before biological fission

Crying Eye's unusual geochemical/nuclear history supports many natural fission provinces. Life first settled their **surroundings**: fluid exchange fronts, mineral interfaces, chemically segregated brines and cooler margins where nuclear and radiogenic processes supplied heat and reactive products. No requirement is imposed that early organic structures lived directly in the hottest reaction zones.

Individual sites are finite. Resource exhaustion, mineral alteration, flow changes, waste accumulation and new exposure shift the geography of productive provinces. Geological renewal exposes existing resources rather than creating unlimited fuel. The current persistence of these provinces is part of the setting's exceptional material history; it is not inferred from ordinary stellar metallicity or from gigapascal confinement alone.

Corion evolution then represents a sequence from **using a nuclear landscape** to **biologically integrating fission-bearing systems**. Large sessile or colonial structures precede sophisticated portable organs. Small Magnetrobes can live around or within such colonies without each 10–50-µm individual being a self-sustaining miniature reactor. Some mature Corion associations become organs of larger hosts; others remain landscape-forming colonies or symbiotic clades. Their exact dimensions, inheritance, nuclear inventory and internal operation are unspecified, not engineered into a technical design here.

## Biochemical foundations

### Gigapascal chemistry and phase-dependent compartments

The principal deep habitats are not merely “high pressure” in a loose sense. A simple reference overburden places **1 GPa at ~94 km**, **2 GPa at ~185 km**, and **3 GPa at ~275 km** below the surface, including a 20-km upper ice lid. The lid base itself is only ~0.184 GPa. These are illustrative depths, not a compressible interior solution or a claim that the whole column is liquid.

Pressure changes the stability and reactivity of water/ammonia/methanol mixtures, mineral phases, dissolved ions and organic frameworks. Organisms depend on permitted local combinations of pressure, temperature and composition. Ice-VI/VII terrain, brine channels, partial melting and interfacial films produce multiple ecological domains. Ice VI and VII are distinct from clathrates; their boundaries need not meet ordinary surface Ice I at one accessible place.

Sessile ancestors use environmental pore networks and phase interfaces for compartmentalization. Mobile descendants evolve **portable organic–mineral boundaries**, ion-selective matrices and internal reaction domains that no longer require permanent attachment to one environmental ice phase. These remain hypothetical alien structures; terrestrial phospholipids, familiar enzymes and DNA/RNA are not assumed.

Supercritical fluids can occur in sufficiently hot geological domains or specifically defined special systems. They are not the ordinary universal cytoplasm of a cold ocean, and pure-water critical constants do not by themselves determine a mixed solvent's phase diagram. Superionic hydrogen clusters are not a required free-floating cytoplasmic constituent.

Some lineages use dissolved metal transformations and mineral precipitation; uranium redox metabolism has terrestrial analogues, but these do not validate the whole alien chemistry. Thorium is not assumed to cycle routinely between aqueous IV/III states, and chemical processing does not erase radioactivity. Nominally ceramic actinide nitrides are not declared inert in wet media; their replacement, passivation or location must respect hydrolysis and pressure chemistry.

### Fission, radiolysis, redox and electricity

Genuine fission supplies energy in geological provinces and Corions. Radioactive decay supplies another source. Radiolysis converts some radiation into chemical products, and other organisms exploit those products without operating their own fission system. This retains a wide ecosystem around the major speculative capability rather than making every organism the same nuclear machine. Terrestrial radiolytic chemistry supports the general energy pathway, not the selected alien organs or biomass. [Radiolysis and subsurface life](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21218-z)

Chemical gradients require separated reactants and replenishment. A static magnetic field is not a consumable fuel. Electrical harvesting may draw from changing fields, conducting-fluid motion or biological/mineral redox circuits, each with a power source and back-reaction. Fission organs can support electrical work, but a nuclear event is not automatically an EMP or an explosion.

A provisional planetary bookkeeping example assigns **10¹³ W of fission heat within 10¹⁴ W total internal heat**, with an illustrative **0.1%** of the former entering biologically available chemical work, or **10¹⁰ W**. Those are declared global design inputs, not measured yields, criticality calculations, proven fuel supplies or a derived organism population. Other geochemical production can contribute separately, with energy counted once. Productive reefs and large consumers concentrate near resource provinces; intervening oceans can be sparse. Localized abundance is not uniform planet-wide lushness.

## Evolutionary overview

The planet/star formation age is **4.5 Gyr**. Biological origin is **2.7 Gyr ago**, approximately **1.8 Gyr after formation**. Initial differentiation and development of ice/brine domains belong to the prebiotic history rather than being biological events at an inconsistent clock zero.

The following broad intervals are a **provisional chronology**, in Myr after biological origin; the numerical boundaries are not independently dated fossils.

| Biological time (Myr) | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 0–400 | Proto-Magnetrobes establish sessile mineral/phase-interface communities around nuclear, magnetic/electrical and thermal exchange provinces |
| 400–700 | Xenon/volatile-release disturbances and environmental losses keep early ecosystems patchy |
| 700–900 | **Great Waste Crisis:** mobile toxic substances, mineral alteration and energetic byproducts overwhelm poorly recycling communities |
| 900–1200 | Ammoniaphages and other consumers exploit, immobilize or export former wastes |
| 1200–1400 | Waste-processing food webs stabilize many provinces; problematic byproducts become resources for other guilds |
| 1400–1700 | **Corions** become biologically integrated fission organs/colonies and later symbiotic modules, beyond merely occupying natural reactor landscapes |
| 1700–2200 | Diversification around Corion-supported and geological fission ecologies; mobility and energy-management adaptations expand |
| 2200–2300 | **Magnetomycota** develop extensive conductive recycling networks in mineral-rich reefs |
| 2300–2450 | Electrical, thermal and fission-associated predator/defense arms races intensify |
| 2450–2550 | **Cryoterrans** colonize shallower interfaces and surface-access corridors, driven by useful carbon and oxidant resources |
| 2550–2700 (present) | Linked deep/surface ecologies persist; armored migrants and their resource exchanges reshape local biogeochemistry |

Carbon Suits are present in the modern biosphere; their precise lineage and emergence date remain unassigned. The natural fission provinces predate biological Corions and must have an adequate resource history throughout the periods in which they support life. Ordinary radioactive decay is not switched on suddenly to explain an evolutionary bloom; access, chemistry and ecology change.

## Trophic classes

### Autotrophs — Magnetrobes and magnetotrophic guilds

Microscopic Magnetrobes are typically **10–50 µm** across and can form meter-scale mats. They anchor at mineral-rich exchange fronts and ice–brine boundaries. Their metabolism couples chemical fixation and ion-gradient management to locally supplied electrical/radiolytic/reactive resources. “Magnetotroph” is a broad functional term for several such lineages, not proof that a stationary field supplies unlimited carbon fixation.

Charge-handling symbionts and later Corion associations can extend these mats into larger integrated communities. Autotrophy still requires carbon, nitrogen and other nutrients; fission power does not manufacture the elemental inventory of a body.

### Advanced producers and symbionts — Corions

Corions comprise specialized clades and biologically integrated organs that genuinely use **nuclear fission**. They evolved from relationships with natural fission provinces and are not reduced to passive decay sources. Sessile/colonial forms can supply surrounding communities; advanced host-associated forms support mobile organisms.

Benefits include sustained local heat, radiolytically generated reactants and energy for chemical/electrical work. Costs include resource acquisition, waste handling, heat rejection, molecular repair and the danger of damaging the host. Regulation is a fictional evolved capability, not an assertion that confinement or biomineralization alone guarantees it. No fuel specifications, core architecture or operating procedures are supplied.

### Consumers — Ammoniaphages

Ammoniaphages range from centimeters to tens of meters across clades, but the largest forms are restricted to sufficiently productive settings. Grazers and predators acquire organic matter, mineral resources and useful chemical gradients. They process uranium-bearing substances, thorium/lead-bearing minerals and other wastes according to their chemistry, not by treating lead as an actinide or all metal ions as interchangeable food.

Mineral sequestration and skeletal deposition can reduce chemical mobility or exposure while retaining residual radioactivity. Chemical immobilization, excretion and transfer to other organisms do not constitute nuclear transmutation or complete detoxification. Their waste-handling role remains central to the Great Waste Crisis's resolution.

### Recyclers — Magnetomycota

Fungal-like, conductive colonies penetrate mineral-rich reef structures and link nearby oxidized/reducing resources through their own living networks. These distribute charge and material **regionally**, with finite resistance, branching, damage, competition and repair. Earth cable bacteria provide an analogy for biological electron transport, not evidence of a lossless planetary circuit. [Living electrical conductors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5984516/)

The reefs recycle resources, modify fluid pathways and transform mineral hot spots into structured habitats. Volatile release follows fluid/geyser/tectonic transport, which biology can influence. A metaphorical “biological magnetospheric pump” is not a field that expels neutral xenon or nitrogen without mechanical work.

### Apex predators — Ignitors

Ignitors carry advanced Corion associations and hazardous, energetically demanding attacks. Their rapid stunning/sensory-disruption effects are primarily **local electrical discharges** or other directly powered mechanisms, rather than nuclear fission automatically producing a remote EMP. Fission-associated radiation and thermal hazards remain real speculative capabilities, with dose, range, shielding, recharge and self-damage constraints.

“Microcriticality” does not require every hunt to be a microscopic nuclear explosion. Corions provide nuclear energy and, in exceptional cases, hazardous nuclear activity; the resulting electrical, thermal and radiological effects are described separately. No trigger, moderator-gating or criticality design is canonized as a technical mechanism.

### Deceptive producers — Fission Lures

Fission Lures are sessile or slow-moving producers that expose or sacrifice attractive resource-bearing appendages. Attacking them can provoke hazardous Corion-associated responses, contaminated material release or powerful local electrical/thermal defenses. Literal fission is preserved in the associated biology, but a brief exposure is not universally or instantly lethal to every predator, and the lure is not invulnerable to its own response.

“Near-critical bursts” describe exceptional activity, not a proven microscale bomb mechanism. Radiation hazards, pressure shocks and immediate stunning have different sources and timescales. Ecological credibility comes from their limited, costly use and differing predator tolerances.

## Surface life — Cryoterrans

Cryoterrans evolved through a **sequence of habitats**: deep exchange provinces, shallower brine reservoirs, ice veins, vent chambers, protected surface niches and finally mobile surface routes. The origin remains sessile and phase-associated; portable boundaries and pressure/temperature tolerance evolved later.

They do not simply ascend while keeping an arbitrary 1–3-GPa environment inside a thin carbon shell. Surface lineages operate with their own adapted internal chemistry and containment requirements. Corions occur only in hosts or associations capable of supporting the corresponding biology; not every small surface scavenger independently carries a complete fission system.

The Cryoplate Wastes supply concentrated tholins, refractory organic material, suitable structural carbon, oxidants and exhumed mineral resources. **Carbon scarcity means scarcity of a usable form or accessible supply**, not absence of every carbon atom. Methanol-rich habitats may not be carbon-limited; other deep basins lack useful organics or structural feedstocks despite having some inorganic carbon. Carbon is also relevant to some Corion-associated structures, but the migration has more than one ecological purpose.

Heavy **graphitic corielles** have nested armor. They serve structural, thermal, chemical and electrical roles, with composite protection against some radiation. Carbon is not universal gamma shielding, and neutron moderation is not the same as safe absorption. Repairable layered structures and behavioral avoidance complement material protection.

### Surface types

- **Phasecreepers:** descendants of phase-interface specialists, following moving freezing fronts, brine veins and suitable mineral/ice contacts. High-pressure forms remain at depth; surface forms do not require Ice VI to be stable in open air.
- **Tholinomorphs:** specialize in processing photochemical products and constructing carbon-rich armor or scaffolds. Their ability to use particular compounds is an evolved chemical capability, not automatic digestion of all tholins.
- **Geyser Herders:** synchronize movements with recurring eruptions, collecting organic concentrates and mineral resources along protected routes. No additional tool use or sapience is implied by the name alone.

Falls, burial, returning organisms and brine transport feed useful surface resources into deeper food webs. Fresh oxidants and photochemical organics can add externally supplied chemical energy; they are not interchangeable with fission fuel.

These are ecological provinces, not a fully mapped latitude-only climate. Dominant boundaries can follow pressure, phase, currents and resource access rather than surface geography.

## Carbon Suits and sapience

**Carbon Suits are sapient native inhabitants.** “Cro-Magnon-equivalent” is interpreted as substantial human-comparable cognitive potential, not an intermediate between animal intelligence and personhood. The “proto-civilizational” descriptor does not establish agriculture, states or industry.

No body shape, exact ancestry, settlement pattern, technological apparatus, pressure-suit mechanism or language is inferred merely from the name. Those details are open. Carbon Suits are not silently merged with every Cryoterran or Geyser Herder.

## Biospheric feedbacks

Life redistributes nuclear-associated mineral resources, chemical reactants and the location of heat release. It can change freezing/melting near colonies, maintain some conduits, block others, promote resource burial and alter charge paths. Fission-associated and electrical disturbances shape competition without making every attack a nuclear detonation.

Magnetomycota link regional habitats, while surface migration returns useful carbon and oxidants. These coupled processes produce a **partial, regional Gaian-style feedback**, with failures, crises and oscillations as well as stabilizing effects. They neither regulate all planetary radioactive decay nor guarantee a globally synchronized living network. Resource depletion, damage costs and heat rejection limit ecological expansion.

## Model limits

The setting deliberately retains both **abundant geological fission provinces** and **literal biological fission Corions**. Their distribution, present nuclear resource conditions, evolved regulation, portability and biological viability are speculative premises requiring further modeling, not verified consequences of Oklo or gigapascal pressure. The life remains chemically alien while its supporting physical accounting is made explicit.
