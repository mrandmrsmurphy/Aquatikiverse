# Gammal System

![[images/Gammal_system_diagram.png]]

*System diagram of Gycklare and Storebror. Illustration is conceptual; numerical labels should follow the values below.*

The **Gammal System** is a fictional system around an old, quiet K-type star toward Sagittarius. Its defining contrast is **a stable local habitat within a less predictable inner-Galactic region**: a visually active mini-Neptune, a strongly magnetized ringed gas giant, and an oceanic moon with native life, an unexplained human arrival, and a later evacuation.

The later, fuller [[Dold-Pärla]] page governs the inhabited-world account. The system keeps the huge planetary sky and short lunar cycle, predominantly refractory rings, a less exposed outer-satellite layout, and a strong but physically qualified magnetic umbrella. The required native-history interval is approximately **four billion years**, not the star’s entire 9.5-Gyr life. The current moon eccentricity is **0.003**.

The system has one known star. Storebror remains the outer known planet. Dold-Pärla is the one named major rounded satellite; smaller moons and minor bodies do not become additional planets.

## Gammal and its environment

Main article: [[Gammal]].

The star’s slow **45-day rotation**, low activity, metal-rich **[Fe/H]≈+0.2** composition and old age provide a quiet local source of illumination. The radius, temperature and luminosity agree. The absolute and apparent magnitudes and extinction are arithmetically consistent; their range describes uncertainty/extinction rather than intrinsic strong variability.

The adopted Earth distance and sightline place the system around **5.3 kpc from the Galactic Center**, not in the immediate nuclear environment. Its inner-disk setting and crowded sky remain. Foreground stars and dust do not establish the local star density, cosmic-ray field or micrometeoroid rate. Galactic hazards may be episodic, and Storebror’s magnetic environment helps create a protected niche, but their actual history requires a model.

The stellar luminosity history and its influence on a four-billion-year biosphere remain uncomputed. Approximate terrestrial HZ references are around **0.58–1.05 AU** for the current star, with strong model dependence. The moon’s eclipses, tides and atmosphere must be evaluated directly rather than declared habitable from a fixed annulus.

## Gycklare

Main article: [[Gycklare]].

Gycklare retains its **6.3 M⊕**, **2.4 R⊕**, 0.22-AU reference. Its derived density is **2.51 g/cm³**, with period **41.6 days** and an edge-on stellar RV amplitude around **1.33 m/s**. It is a warm/hot mini-Neptune interior to the habitable region, with a heavy-element-dominated interior and an optically thick H/He atmosphere rather than a demonstrated majority-gas mass budget.

The design goal is **churn and visual change**: bands, vortices, wave patterns and patchy pale-blue/white haze. Temperature varies with pressure and circulation. Full-redistribution equilibrium is about **414 K** at Bond albedo 0.3; layer temperatures of 670 K or above 900 K are possible in a model but are not unqualified equilibrium values.

The high escape estimate **7×10¹³ kg/year** remains an uncertain or episodic high state pending an XUV model. A UV tail is a target, not a verified permanent feature of specified size. The automated circa-2439-CE transit/RV discovery and Swedish “jester/trickster” naming remain within the fictional chronology.

## Storebror

Main article: [[Storebror]].

The ringed **2.2-M♃**, **1.1-R♃** giant orbits at 0.572 AU with about **174.3-day** period. The density is **2.05 g/cm³**, reference gravity **45.1 m/s²**, escape speed **84.2 km/s**, and stellar RV amplitude about **91 m/s**. The **89.8°** observer inclination allows the intended transit; 88° would not.

Storebror rotates in **9.7 hours** and has a low **5° spin obliquity** in the working reference. A flattening target around **0.04**, rather than 0.025, is consistent with a simple positive-J₂ rotating-fluid estimate; the actual interior is not solved. The atmospheric inventory retains H₂, He, ammonia, water vapor and photochemical minor species. Normal stellar input is **1414 W/m²**; a globe Bond albedo 0.35 gives approximately **252 K equilibrium**.

### Bright rings and the satellite family

The bright rings are **predominantly reflective refractory debris**, with transient/protected ice and possible recent disruption or ongoing ejecta supply. Dense C/B/A bands and a faint extension to **250,000 km / 3.18 Rp** replace an unexplained uniformly ancient icy disk. Minut and Zotra retain paired shepherding of the narrow A-band; their neighboring orbits are not automatically a horseshoe/coorbital exchange.

**Ajaga** remains volcanic and rocky. **Saxa** is a carbonaceous retrograde irregular at 2.1 million km with revised e≈0.15. **Trasp** remains a tumbling captured irregular, now a more compact, lower-eccentricity, highly retrograde candidate at **3.2 million km, e≈0.08**, rather than a nearly polar 5.2-million-km orbit reaching the Hill boundary. Its period is about **24.94 days**. Dold-Pärla’s more detailed physical and historical account takes priority over the preliminary Storebror table.

The other five moons remain unspecified members of the eleven-moon census. Their sizes, masses and orbits must be supplied before a complete stability claim. Storebror’s Hill scale is about **8.1 million km**, not 10.5 million; simply being inside it is not sufficient for every inclination/eccentricity.

### A major magnetic difference from Earth

The setting adopts a **3-mT equatorial field**—about seven representative Jovian equatorial fields—and a pressure-variable magnetopause around **30 Rp** in an illustrative environment. Dold-Pärla orbits well inside it at 6.1 Rp and also has a reference intrinsic field around **30 μT** plus a near-Earth-pressure atmosphere.

This nested environment preserves the intended strong charged-particle protection and conspicuous magnetic/auroral character. The reported low surface radiation is an **additional constraint**, not something derived from a large field alone. Radiation belts, photon attenuation and the high-energy cosmic-ray tail still require transport/dose calculations. Magnetic fields do not provide orbital stability or stop every form of Galactic radiation.

## Dold-Pärla

Main article: [[Dold-Pärla]].

Dold-Pärla retains **0.9 M⊕**, **0.95 R⊕**, **77% ocean coverage**, robust tectonics, a near-Earth nitrogen–oxygen atmosphere and a **288-K** reference surface climate. Its corrected gravity is about **9.79 m/s²**. An explicit illustrative one-bar dry atmosphere implements the supplied near-Earth premise without implying identical air through all eras.

### Sky and climate

At **480,000 km**, the moon’s period/spin is **1.448 days** and its prograde stellar cycle about **1.460 days**. Storebror spans **18.9°**. Its nearly equatorial orbit and the giant’s small spin tilt reconcile “equatorial” with a several-degree inclination to the star’s plane. Both hemispheres receive stellar day/night, while only the near hemisphere sees the nearly fixed giant.

Long eclipses near every stellar conjunction remain, with approximately **1.6 hours** as a representative substantial occultation rather than a universal contact duration. The rings generally appear narrow from this almost edge-on vantage; brighter satellites are moving points. Planetshine changes with phase and gives striking night illumination without being the principal global heat source. Annual geometry, precession and weather have distinct periods; no unsupported multi-week eclipse clock is assigned.

The chosen **e=0.003** and illustrative lunar **k₂/Q=3×10⁻⁴** give about **2.4 W/m²** internal heat. The total illustrative absorbed/internal budget is about **232–233 W/m²**, compatible with an effective temperature around 253 K and a roughly 35-K greenhouse to the 288-K target. This is not a climate demonstration. Cloud feedbacks, tides, stellar history and ocean circulation remain research tasks.

### Four billion years, not a permanently frozen orbit

The required **four-billion-year native interval** can be represented by an analytic weak-primary-tide branch moving from about **413,000 to 480,000 km**. Its **Qp/k₂p≈3×10⁷** is a deliberately weak, **unvalidated response requirement**, not a measurement or an automatic property of a strongly magnetized giant. It must be justified by an interior/frequency-dependent model before this counts as a demonstrated long-lived configuration.

The present eccentricity has a much shorter damping timescale in the illustrative lunar model. It need not have persisted at 0.003 for four billion years; possible recent forcing is compatible with—but not proved by—the Archive’s satellite anomalies. Neither a catastrophic moon relocation nor an unknown engineering mechanism is inserted as an explanation.

Three **100-year four-body snapshot screens** and a **25-year timestep comparison** found no immediate lunar disruption in the selected phases. They omit the other moons, tides, oblateness and long-term evolution, and do **not** validate four billion years. The setup and results are under Physical basis.

## Biosphere, people and evacuation

Native life uses carbon-based, DNA/RNA-like molecular structures and is chemically compatible with human biology despite the setting’s independent origin. Forests, reefs, trenches and aerial arthropod analogs coexist with **eclipse-blooming bioluminescent fungal analogs**, **eusocial symbol-using herbivores**, and marine life exploiting tidal thermal/redox microenvironments.

The **Unified Cycle Archive, Rev. 9.4410**, reconstructs a sudden human appearance around **100,000 years before its reference epoch**, from **three male and nine female founders**, without precursor hominids or a native human lineage. Present viability and interfertility, local food/air compatibility, enhanced night vision/magnetoreception, cortical branching and modest radiation/hypoxia tolerance are retained. Their exact origins and the demographic viability of the founder reconstruction remain part of the mystery; no intervention is invented to explain them.

Associated minor-satellite anomalies and a coherent magnetic-mineral structure remain unresolved observations. “Gammal–Storebror flux-axis” alignment is phase-referenced, not a star–planet line somehow fixed forever on the moon. No creator, transport method or cause is named without an observational record.

The sequence ends in **evacuation after the human era**. The cause, date, destination, completion status and aftermath remain unspecified. The article records the inhabited-world era; it does not imply either that every settlement is still occupied or that the moon has become sterile. Earth’s circa-2439 system discovery, local antiquity, the Archive’s cycles and evacuation need a later calendar crosswalk.

## Open modeling tasks

The open work is quantitative modeling or unspecified narrative detail:

1. **Long-term habitability:** justify the weak primary tidal response, reconstruct the moon’s origin and four-billion-year orbital/thermal history, include stellar luminosity and giant spin/radius evolution, and identify present eccentricity forcing. Do not treat the analytic branch as a fitted physical model.
2. **Full satellite system:** give the five unnamed moons physical/orbital properties; integrate the eleven-moon layout with J₂, inclinations, resonances and tides. Check Ajaga heating, shepherd torques, Trasp/Saxa secular behavior, ring aggregation and debris replenishment.
3. **Magnetic protection:** calculate the pressure-dependent field/current geometry, energetic-belt loading, lunar atmospheric column, cosmic-ray access and photon/particle dose. Preserve the low-dose inhabited setting as a constraint while testing its implementation.
4. **Climate and weather:** model eclipse contacts and duty cycle in the tilted geometry; couple cloud albedo, 77%-ocean transport, atmospheric greenhouse, variable planetshine and tidal heat. Gycklare needs its own cloud/circulation/XUV-escape model to realize the desired churn and appearance.
5. **Human mystery and biology:** establish the evidence behind the twelve-founder inference, demographic/genetic history, molecular/nutritional compatibility and measured adaptations. Keep the origin unresolved unless additional evidence is supplied; native taxonomy/physiology is not yet fully described.
6. **Evacuation:** specify trigger, dates/reference present, destinations, completion and ecological aftermath. No cause or catastrophe has been assumed. Clarify the relationship of Archive cycles, local antiquity and CE discovery dates.
7. **Background references and visuals:** define the map coordinate basis, Galactic trajectory, wind/astropause and formation snow-line epoch. Existing illustrations are conceptual, and their diagrams and brightness should not be mistaken for numerical models.

## Physical basis

The calculations support arithmetic consistency and expose physical requirements. They do not prove a four-billion-year habitable system or explain the unexplained human arrival and later evacuation. The two major planets are separated by about 9.3 mutual Hill radii in a near-circular coplanar reference, which is a screen, not a long-term stability proof. The stellar, giant, moon and climate calculations are on [[Gammal]], [[Storebror]], [[Dold-Pärla]] and [[Gycklare]] under Physical basis.

**Four-body screen.** A separate Newtonian screen includes Gammal, Gycklare, Storebror and Dold-Pärla, in units of AU, Julian years and solar masses (G = 4π²), with masses of 0.82, 6.3×3.0034896×10⁻⁶, 2.2×0.0009545942 and 0.9×3.0034896×10⁻⁶ M☉ (these rounded conversions differ slightly from the SI constants and do not change the narrative precision). The initial axes and eccentricities are Gycklare 0.22 AU / 0.005, the giant–moon barycenter 0.572 AU / 0.005 and the moon 480,000 km / 0.003; the planets are coplanar, the moon is inclined 5° and the giant has no modeled spin quadrupole. The seeds j = 0, 1, 2 use planetary mean anomalies of 0.4 + 0.734j and 1.7 + 0.411j radians and a lunar mean anomaly of 2.3999632297j radians, with the giant–moon subsystem initialized about its barycenter and the whole system shifted to zero barycentric position and velocity. The integration uses direct pair gravity with a fourth-order symmetric velocity-Verlet composition (w₁ = 1/(2 − 2^(1/3)), w₀ = −2^(1/3)/(2 − 2^(1/3))) at a main step h = 10⁻⁵ yr, sampling radius and eccentricity every 25 steps and the total-energy error every 2,500 steps. The run stops if the moon–planet separation falls below 250,000 km or rises above 900,000 km, a conservative local screen, not the actual Roche or Hill boundary.

The step comparison gives consistent short-interval radius and eccentricity scales, but it is not a formal long-term convergence result, and the osculating eccentricity includes forced variations, so it should not be inserted directly into an averaged heat formula. The screen excludes all other moons, ring gravity and collisions, J₂ and spin, tides, migration, stellar evolution, Galactic encounters, radiation and climate. It supports only the selected geometry; it does not certify Trasp, eleven-moon stability, four Gyr of orbital survival or a four-Gyr biosphere.
