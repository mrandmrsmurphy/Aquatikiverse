# Reading paths

Four ordered tours, one for each way into the Aquatikiverse. Each starts with the shortest reference and moves toward the strangest worlds. Return to [[Home]] to switch paths.

## Physics

1. [[Stars]]: spectral types, the main sequence, habitable zones, binaries.
2. [[Planets and orbits]]: Kepler's law, flux and temperature, tides, stability.
3. [[Sol System]]: the real solar system with sourced numbers, the yardstick for every other world.
4. [[Middle Path]]: a tidally locked planet around an active red dwarf; its Physical basis covers climate, atmosphere, circulation and dynamo.
5. [[Stellula System]] and [[Methuselan System]]: tidal heating, eclipses and spin.
6. [[Skemer]]: a rogue world and its coupled tidal model.
7. [[Gammal System]] and [[Trigeminal System]]: multi-body stability screens, up to six stars.
8. [[Lytherin System]]: a deliberately manufactured architecture of worlds and moons.
9. [[Tom (black hole)|Tom]] and [[Sagittarius A*]]: black holes as hosts.

## Chemistry

1. [[Redox Chemistry]]: donors, acceptors, the energy ledger and a nine-step method for testing a reaction set.
2. [[Life on Goku]] and [[Life on Chronmismer]]: familiar water and oxygen as the baseline.
3. [[Life on Aphrod]] and [[Life on Azotia]]: ammonia–water solvents.
4. [[Life on Thionis]], [[Life on Stygium]] and [[Life on Iodulon]]: liquid sulfur dioxide and halide chemistry.
5. [[Life on Dysmorph]]: halide liquids and uranium redox.
6. [[Life on Lunara]]: radiolysis as an energy source.
7. [[Glaciumite]]: a mineral and its formation and extraction physics.

## Speculative biology

1. [[Biospheres]]: the table of every biosphere by solvent and metabolism.
2. [[Life on Magnum]], [[Life on Nibbles]] and [[Life on Neonova]]: water-based life under unusual light, aurorae and pressure.
3. [[Life on Middle Path]]: a silicon biosphere beside transplanted Earth life.
4. [[Life on Crying Eye]]: fission-powered Corions beneath ice.
5. [[Life on Alchemis]] and [[Life on Jaspis]]: life with no bulk liquid.
6. [[Life on Oxhydra]] and [[Pumper Beetles]]: episodic water and a notable species.
7. [[Life on Skemer]] and [[Skemer-mense]]: life on a rogue world and its human descendants.

## Worlds and stories

1. [[Aquatikiverse]]: the atlas of systems.
2. [[Ɬiʔa System]] and [[Middle Path]]: the central setting.
3. [[Aethonix System]]: a mature, settled binary system with eight planets.
4. [[Lytherin Empire]]: the empire of the nine inhabited moons.
5. [[Altmania System]]: six biosphere worlds and the human colonies on Nasia and Dunamas.
6. [[Nimvion System]]: a veiled system with the Morphfiends.
7. [[Orpheus and Eurydice System]]: three worlds around a stellar pair, with the Formoquens.
